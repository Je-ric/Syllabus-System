# Module 3 — Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

**Who manages this:** Admin and Chair

---

## What It Is

PEOs describe what graduates are expected to achieve several years after graduation. These are career and professional accomplishment statements — the long-term outcomes your program produces.

---

## Where to Find It

1. Go to **Programs** from the sidebar
2. Select your program using the program selector
3. Click the **Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)** tab

---

## Unsaved Changes Bar

When you add or modify PEOs, an amber bar appears at the top showing how many rows are new or modified. This is a reminder that your changes have not been saved yet. Click **Save All** to apply them.

---

## How to Add a PEO

1. Click **Add PEO** — a new blank row appears at the bottom
2. Type the PEO description in the text area
3. Repeat to add more PEOs
4. Click **Save All** when done

> You cannot add another row if the current blank row is still empty. Fill it in first.

---

## How to Edit a PEO

1. Click directly into the text area of the PEO you want to change
2. Update the text — the row turns amber to indicate unsaved changes
3. Click **Save All** to apply

---

## How to Remove an Unsaved Row

If you added a row but want to discard it before saving, click the **X button** on that row. This only removes rows that have not been saved yet.

---

## How to Delete a Saved PEO

1. Click the **trash icon** on the saved PEO row
2. A confirmation dialog appears — click **Delete** to confirm

> Deletion is permanent. All PEO codes are re-sequenced automatically after deletion.

---

## How to Revert Unsaved Changes

If you have made changes but have not saved yet, click the **Revert** button to discard all unsaved additions and edits. Saved rows return to their original text.

---

## Saving

- Click **Save All** to save all new and modified PEOs at once
- A confirmation dialog appears before saving — click **Save All** to confirm
- PEO codes (a, b, c… aa, ab…) are assigned automatically based on the order they were saved

---

## Notes

- Admins can manage PEOs for any program
- Chairs can only manage PEOs for programs in their assigned department
- PEO codes are re-assigned every time you save — the order in the list determines the codes

---

## Example

> PEO a — "Graduates will be employed in leadership roles in agriculture-related industries."
> PEO b — "Graduates will engage in lifelong learning and professional development."
