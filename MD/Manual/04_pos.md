# Module 4 — Program Outcomes (POs)

**Who manages this:** Admin and Chair

---

## What It Is

Program Outcomes describe the specific skills, knowledge, and attitudes students must demonstrate by the time they graduate. Each PO can also be linked to one or more PEOs to show how graduation skills connect to long-term career outcomes.

---

## Where to Find It

1. Go to **Programs** from the sidebar
2. Select your program using the program selector
3. Click the **Program Outcomes (POs)** tab

---

## Unsaved Changes Bar

When you add or modify POs, an amber bar appears showing how many rows are new or modified. This is a reminder that text changes have not been saved yet. Click **Save All** to apply.

> PEO mapping (linking POs to PEOs) saves immediately when you check or uncheck a chip — it does not wait for Save All.

---

## How to Add a PO

1. Click **Add PO** — a new blank row appears
2. Type the PO description in the text area
3. Repeat to add more POs
4. Click **Save All** when done

> You cannot add another row if the current blank row is still empty. Fill it in first.

---

## How to Edit a PO

1. Click into the text area of the PO you want to change
2. Update the text — the row turns amber to show unsaved changes
3. Click **Save All** to apply

---

## How to Remove an Unsaved Row

Click the **X button** on the row. This only removes rows not yet saved.

---

## How to Delete a Saved PO

1. Click the **trash icon** on the row
2. A confirmation dialog appears — click **Delete** to confirm

> A PO cannot be deleted if it is already used in a syllabus course outcome. You must remove it from the syllabus first.

---

## How to Map a PO to PEOs

Each saved PO row has a **Maps to PEOs** section at the bottom showing a chip for each PEO defined in the program.

1. Click a PEO chip to link that PO to the PEO — it turns green when active
2. Click it again to unlink
3. Mapping saves immediately — no need to click Save All

> Mapping chips are only active on saved PO rows. Save the PO first before mapping.

To view the full text of each PEO while mapping, click the **View PEOs** link on any row — it opens a side panel with all PEOs listed.

---

## How to Revert Unsaved Changes

Click the **Revert** button to discard all unsaved text additions and edits. PEO mapping changes are already saved and are not affected by Revert.

---

## Saving

- Click **Save All** to save all new and modified PO text at once
- A confirmation dialog appears — click **Save All** to confirm
- PO codes (a, b, c… aa, ab…) are assigned automatically in list order

---

## Matrix View

After setting up both PEOs and POs, click the **Matrix View** tab to see a read-only grid showing which POs are mapped to which PEOs. This is useful for reviewing your program's outcome alignment at a glance.

---

## Notes

- Admins can manage POs for any program
- Chairs can only manage POs for programs in their assigned department

---

## Example

> PO a — "Apply knowledge of mathematics and science to solve complex agricultural problems."
> → Linked to: PEO a, PEO b

> PO b — "Conduct investigations using research-based methods."
> → Linked to: PEO b
