# Module 2 — Department Objectives

**Who manages this:** Admin and Chair

---

## What It Is

Department Objectives are specific, measurable outcomes that support college goals. They translate broad college aspirations into actionable targets for your department.

---

## Before You Start

> If you are a Chair and see a "No department assigned" warning, your account has not been assigned to a department yet. Contact your administrator before proceeding.

---

## Selecting a College and Department

The page has two dropdowns — college first, then department.

- **Admin** — can select any college, then any department within it
- **Chair** — your assigned college and department are selected automatically. You cannot switch to another department.

The Add Objective button only appears after both a college and a department are selected.

---

## How to Add an Objective

1. Go to **Department Objectives** from the sidebar
2. Select your college from the first dropdown (admin only)
3. Select your department from the second dropdown (admin only)
4. Click **Add Objective**
5. Type the objective description in the text area
6. Click **Add Objective** to save

---

## How to Edit an Objective

1. Click the **Edit icon** (pencil) next to the objective
2. Update the description in the modal that opens
3. Click **Save Changes**

---

## How to Delete an Objective

1. Click the **Delete icon** (trash) next to the objective
2. Read the confirmation — it shows the objective text and warns the action cannot be undone
3. Click **Delete Objective** to confirm

> After deletion, all remaining objectives are automatically re-sequenced (a, b, c… aa, ab…).

---

## Notes

- Objective codes are auto-assigned in alphabetical order after saving
- You cannot add objectives until both a college and a department are selected
- Codes re-sequence automatically whenever an objective is deleted

---

## Example

> Objective a — "Develop research-driven instructional materials by the end of the academic year."
> Objective b — "Increase student engagement in industry-based projects."
