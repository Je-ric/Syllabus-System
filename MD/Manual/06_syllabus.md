# Module 6 — Syllabus Creation

**Who creates syllabi:** Faculty

---

## What It Is

A syllabus is the complete teaching guide for a course in a given semester. It covers instructor details, weekly topics, learning outcomes, assessment tasks, and grading weights. The system walks you through creation using a 6-step wizard. Progress is saved automatically — you can leave and return at any time.

---

## My Syllabi Page

Go to **Syllabi** from the sidebar to see all syllabi you have created, organized into four tabs:

| Tab | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Draft | Syllabi still in progress — you can continue editing |
| Under Review | Syllabi submitted and waiting for review |
| For Revision | Syllabi returned to you with comments |
| Approved | Completed and approved syllabi |

Each card shows the course code, academic year, program, and your current progress step. Use the **Continue** or **View** button to open a syllabus, and the **Preview** button to open the read-only view.

---

## How to Start a New Syllabus

1. Click **Create Syllabus** from the My Syllabi page
2. Select your program from the program selector
3. Browse courses listed by Year Level and Semester
4. Click **Create Syllabus** on the course row

> If a course shows **"No PO mapped"** instead of the Create button, it has no Program Outcome mappings. You cannot create a syllabus for it until the department chair maps POs to the course.

> If you already have a syllabus for that course, the wizard will open your existing one instead of creating a new one.

---

## Step 1 — Academic Calendar

Select which semester this syllabus is for.

1. Choose a semester from the dropdown (e.g., AY 2024-2025 — 1st Semester)
2. The selection saves automatically as soon as you choose

The calendar determines your semester dates, which weeks are exam weeks, which are holidays, and which are non-teaching. All of this shapes the Weekly Coverage step.

> Changing the calendar after weeks have been generated will require you to regenerate all weeks — this deletes all existing weekly content.

---

## Step 2 — Course Components

Enter instructor details and class schedules. For LEC+LAB courses, both components appear.

### Instructor Details

The following fields are pre-filled from your profile:

- Instructor Name
- Instructor Email
- Phone (optional)
- Office

Update them directly on this step if needed.

### Class Hours and Passing Mark

These are read-only — they are pulled from the course settings and cannot be changed here.

### Class Schedule

1. Click **Add** next to Class Schedule
2. Select the day (Monday through Friday)
3. Type the time (e.g., 07:30 AM – 09:00 AM)
4. Click the **trash icon** on any row to remove it

For LEC+LAB courses, fill in the schedule for both the LEC and LAB sections separately.

### Consultation Hours

1. Click **Edit** next to Consultation Hours
2. A modal opens — add rows with day and time
3. Click **Save** in the modal

Consultation hours are saved to your profile and shared across all your syllabi.

### Saving

Click **Save All** at the bottom when you are done with this step.

---

## Step 3 — Course Outcomes

Define what students will be able to do after completing this course.

On the right side you will see a **Course Information drawer** button and a **Program Outcomes reference drawer** button — use these to review the course details and the POs mapped to this course while writing your outcomes.

### Unsaved Changes Bar

When you add or modify outcomes, an amber bar appears at the top. Your changes are staged — click **Save All** to apply them.

### How to Add Course Outcomes

1. Click **Add Course Outcome** — a new blank row appears
2. Type the outcome description
3. Repeat to add more
4. Click **Save All** when done

> You cannot add another row if the current blank row is empty. Fill it in first.

Course Outcomes are automatically coded CO1, CO2, CO3, and so on.

### How to Edit an Outcome

Click into the text area of the outcome, make your change, then click **Save All**.

### How to Remove an Unsaved Outcome

Click the **X button** on the row to discard it before saving.

### How to Delete a Saved Outcome

1. Click the **trash icon** on the row
2. A confirmation dialog appears — click **Delete** to confirm

Remaining outcomes are automatically re-sequenced after deletion.

### Revert

Click **Revert** to discard all unsaved changes and restore saved rows to their last saved text.

> You must have at least one saved Course Outcome before you can submit the syllabus.

---

## Step 4 — Weekly Coverage

Structure the entire semester week by week.

### Generate Weeks

Click **Generate Weekly Coverage**. The system creates weekly rows based on the academic calendar you selected.

| Week Type | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Always MVGO (Mission, Vision, Goals & Objectives) — content is fixed |
| Exam weeks | Locked — automatically labeled (e.g., Midterm Exam, Final Exam) |
| Break weeks | Skipped entirely — no row is created for these |
| Non-teaching weeks | Locked — automatically labeled |

### Filling in a Week

Weeks are shown as collapsed rows. Each week has four content panels:

- Unit Learning Outcomes
- Assessment Task
- Topics
- Teaching & Learning Activities

Click the **Edit Week** button (or the edit icon on any individual panel) to open the **week edit modal**. Inside the modal:

1. Select the **Course Outcome** this week is linked to (skip for MVGO weeks)
2. Fill in Unit Learning Outcomes, Topics, Teaching & Learning Activities, and Assessment Task using the rich text editor
3. Add **References** — click Add Reference, type the reference text
4. Add **Online Materials** — click Add Material, enter the name and URL
5. Click **Save** in the modal to apply

The week saves when you close the modal. You can also click **Save All** on the step to save all weeks at once.

### Resetting a Week

Click **Reset** on any editable week to clear all its content. A confirmation is required.

> Resetting a week is permanent — all content for that week is deleted.

### Regenerating Weeks

If you change the academic calendar or need to rebuild the week structure, click **Regenerate Weeks**.

> Regenerating weeks deletes all existing weekly content. A confirmation is required before this happens.

### LEC and LAB Tabs

If your course has both LEC and LAB, two tabs appear at the top of the weekly coverage section. You must fill in coverage for each component separately by switching between the **LEC** and **LAB** tabs.

---

## Step 5 — Course Evaluation

Assign a percentage weight to each assessment task. The total must reach the correct target.

1. The table lists every week that has an assessment task
2. Enter the weight for each row in the **LEC** column (whole numbers only, e.g., 10 for 10%)
3. For LEC+LAB courses, also fill in the **LAB** column
4. The running total updates as you type — watch the badge at the bottom of each column
5. Click **Save** when done

### Weight Targets

| Course Type | LEC Must Total | LAB Must Total |
|---|---|---|
| LEC only | 100% | — |
| LEC + LAB | 67% | 33% |

The badge turns green when you hit the exact target and red when you are off.

> Week 1 (MVGO) does not appear in the evaluation table — it is not assessable.

## Step 6 — Review and Preview

The final step before submitting your syllabus.

### Syllabus Previews

Three preview formats are available — each opens in a new tab:

| Format | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Complete | Full syllabus document |
| Abridged | Condensed version |
| Assessment Plan | Evaluation summary |

### Revision History

If this syllabus is a revised version of a previous one, you can record the revision history here.

1. Click **Add Revision**
2. A modal opens — fill in:
   - **Date** — required
   - **Rev. No.** — a whole number starting from 0
   - **Implementation Semester** — required (e.g., 1st Sem 2025-2026)
   - **Highlights** — brief summary of changes (optional)
   - **Contributors** — names of contributors (optional)
3. Click **Add Revision** in the modal to save

To edit a saved revision, click the **edit icon** on its row and update in the modal.
To delete, click the **trash icon** on the row.
If you have more than one revision, click **Resequence** to renumber them 0, 1, 2, … in their current order.

### Review and Approval

This section has two parts: Signatories and Additional Reviewers.

#### Signatories

- **Prepared By** — your name is shown automatically (read-only)
- **Approved By** — select a dean from the dropdown, then click **Set**
- **Concurred By** — select a different dean from the dropdown, then click **Set**
  - The same person cannot be both Approved By and Concurred By

To remove a signatory, click the **X button** on their name card.

#### Additional Reviewers

You can add faculty members as additional reviewers — they will appear in the printed syllabus signature section.

1. Select a faculty member from the dropdown
2. Click **Add**
3. The reviewer appears in the list

To remove a reviewer, click the **trash icon** on their row.

### Submitting

When everything is complete, click **Submit for Review** at the bottom of the page.

> Once submitted, the syllabus moves to "Under Review" and you can no longer edit it until it is returned for revision.

Before submitting, make sure:
- An academic calendar is selected
- Course components are filled in
- At least one Course Outcome is saved
- At least one week exists
- Course evaluation weights are complete and totals are correct

---

## Managing Existing Syllabi

From the **My Syllabi** page:

- **Draft** — click **Continue** to return to the wizard at your last step
- **Under Review** — click **View** to see the syllabus (read-only)
- **For Revision** — click **Continue** to edit and resubmit
- **Approved** — click **View** to see the final approved syllabus
