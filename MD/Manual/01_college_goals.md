# Module 1 — College Goals

**Who manages this:** Admin and Dean

---

## What It Is

College Goals define the outcomes your college aims to achieve. These are broad institutional statements that guide all academic activities within the college.

---

## Before You Start

> If you are a Dean and see a "No college assigned" warning, your account has not been assigned to a college yet. Contact your administrator before proceeding.

---

## Selecting a College

- **Admin** — a college dropdown is shown. Select a college to view its goals.
- **Dean** — your assigned college is selected automatically. You cannot switch to another college.

The Add Goal button only appears after a college is selected.

---

## How to Add a Goal

1. Go to **College Goals** from the sidebar
2. Select your college (admin only — deans see theirs automatically)
3. Click **Add Goal**
4. Type the goal description in the text area
5. Click **Add Goal** to save

---

## How to Edit a Goal

1. Click the **Edit icon** (pencil) next to the goal
2. Update the description in the modal that opens
3. Click **Save Changes**

---

## How to Delete a Goal

1. Click the **Delete icon** (trash) next to the goal
2. Read the confirmation — it will show you the goal text and warn you the action cannot be undone
3. Click **Delete Goal** to confirm

> After deletion, all remaining goals are automatically re-sequenced (a, b, c… aa, ab…).

---

## Notes

- Goal codes are auto-assigned in alphabetical order after saving
- You cannot add goals until a college is selected
- Codes re-sequence automatically whenever a goal is deleted

---

## Example

> Goal a — "Produce globally competitive graduates in agricultural sciences."
> Goal b — "Foster a culture of research and innovation within the college."
