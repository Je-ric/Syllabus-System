A. Document Control and Course Information  
1. The official syllabus template is used.  
2. All required course details are complete and correct.  
3. The syllabus shows the version number and date of  
preparation or revision.  
4. Submission is correctly identiﬁed as Updating or Revision  
B. Course Outcomes and OBE Alignment  
1. Course Outcomes are SMART (speciﬁc, measurable,  
achievable, relevant, and time-bound).  
2. The number of Course Outcomes follows institutional policy,  
preferably three (3) Course Outcomes. If more than three  
Course Outcomes are used, a written justiﬁcation is  
attached.  
3. Course Outcomes are clearly mapped to the appropriate  
Program Outcomes.  
4. Course content is aligned with the stated Course Outcomes.  
5. Teaching-learning activities are aligned with the stated  
Course Outcomes.  
6. Assessment tasks are aligned with the stated Course  
Outcomes.  
C. Speciﬁc Compliance for UPDATING  
1. The proposed changes are minor, routine, and do not  
substantially alter the course design.  
2. No change was made to the Course Outcomes.  
3. No change was made to the grading system.  
4. No change was made to the core course content.
